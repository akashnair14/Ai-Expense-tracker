Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Zero Data Selling Commitment

Kinetiq Money will never sell, rent, monetize, or share your financial records, transaction amounts, or personal identities with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Kinetiq Money (via Telegram Bot or WebApp), we store only what is necessary to categorize and calculate your financial health:

  • Account Identifiers: Telegram User ID, username, and first name (or email address if registered directly).
  • Transaction Data: Amounts, merchants, categories, dates, and recurring frequency parameters you submit.
  • Personalization Targets: Monthly spending budgets, income benchmarks, and savings goals you configure.

2. How AI is Used

Kinetiq Money utilizes Google Gemini 1.5 Flash Vision and deterministic NLU regex models exclusively to extract transaction parameters (e.g. amount, merchant, category) from your messages or receipt uploads. Prompts and receipt images are processed strictly for extraction and are not retained for external LLM public training.

3. Security & Storage

All database records are hosted within secure, encrypted PostgreSQL infrastructure with SSL-enforced connections. API communications use HTTPS with rate limiting and JWT session authorization.

4. Your Data Rights & Deletion

You have full ownership of your data at all times. You can:

  • Export: Download a full timestamped CSV of your records at any time.
  • Delete: Delete individual records with immediate confirmation warnings or clear historical balances.

5. Contact & Support

For questions or assistance regarding your data, reach out directly through the official Kinetiq Money Telegram Bot support channels.
