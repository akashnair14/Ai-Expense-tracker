Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to master Kinetiq Money

1. How do I record expenses using natural language?

Simply send any natural message in Telegram or web quick add, like "spent 450 on swiggy", "uber cab 320", or "salary 50000 credited". Kinetiq Money automatically extracts the merchant, amount, category, and date in <500ms.

2. How is my Pulse Health Score (0-100) calculated?

Pulse Health evaluates your net savings retention (up to 40 pts), monthly category budget adherence (up to 40 pts), and cash flow stability (up to 20 pts). Scores >80 represent excellent financial health.

3. What is Safe Daily Spend Limit?

It dynamically divides your remaining discretionary budget by the exact number of days left in the current calendar month. Staying under this limit guarantees you will not overspend.

4. How do recurring commitments (SIP, Rent, EMI) work?

Configure any fixed outlay with a target day of the month (e.g. Day 1 for Rent). Our automated cron background service logs it on the exact day and adjusts your monthly projections in real time.

5. Is my financial data private and exportable?

Yes, 100%. We never sell your personal data. You can download a complete CSV spreadsheet of your transactions at any time or review our Privacy Policy.
